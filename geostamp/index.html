GeoStamp – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the GeoStamp mobile application (the "App") developed by GCG (the "Developer", "we", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Access

To provide its core features (adding location and metadata stamps to photos), the App may access:

  • Location data: GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) used to render the location on the photo stamp.
  • Camera & photos: Capture of photos and saving of the resulting stamped images on your device.
  • Address & weather: We query third-party services to fetch an address (BigDataCloud) and current weather (Open-Meteo) based on your coordinates.
  • Device orientation: Sensor data used only to display the level indicator in the camera view.

The App does not collect your name, email address, contacts, or any other personal identifiers unless you voluntarily contact us.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide the features above, the App sends your coordinates to the following third-party services:

  • BigDataCloud – reverse geocoding (coordinates to address).
    bigdatacloud.com/privacy
  • Open-Meteo – current weather data.
    open-meteo.com/en/terms
  • OpenStreetMap – static map tiles rendered on the stamp.
    osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Sentry (optional, if enabled) – anonymized crash reports to improve stability.
    sentry.io/privacy

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage & Security

All captured photos, coordinates, and generated stamps are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server controlled by us. Network requests to third-party services are performed over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Permissions

  • CAMERA – required to take photos.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION – required to add GPS coordinates to the stamp.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) – required to show a notification after a photo is saved.

You can revoke any permission at any time from your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke location and camera permissions at any time. For any question about your data, contact us at: fa.stiman3@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
